What to deliver

Filming essentials

  • 60 seconds · Native 9:16 · 4K resolution (2160 × 3840).
  • Appear in the introduction, personal evaluation and conclusion. Full-screen product close-ups, app demonstrations and lifestyle B-roll are encouraged. Record clear English audio.
  • Create a scene that fits your own routine. Daytime filming is welcome; use a nighttime setting only when it suits a night-rest story. Keep the face and product clearly lit.
  • Show the benefits through actual use in polished, well-lit settings.
  • Confirm that the video may be used as BURTRAN advertising material.
  • Within 14 days of receiving the product, send the video preview and at least five high-resolution original photos. BURTRAN will provide review feedback. Submit any agreed revisions before publishing.
  • Obtain written approval before publishing. Review covers factual accuracy, brand assets, disclosure and agreed production requirements. Honest limitations and opinions do not require a positive verdict. Submit factual corrections before publishing.
  • Shoot in portrait orientation and fill the entire 9:16 frame. Horizontal video inserted into a vertical frame is not acceptable. Do not use black bars, blurred backgrounds or decorative borders to fill unused space. This applies to every shot, including product close-ups and split-screen layouts.

Test and record before filming your final review

This is an experience-based review, not an unboxing alone. Before testing, confirm the supplied sample, app and firmware are on the brand-approved version and apply any supplied update instructions. Record actual use first, then turn your observations into the video. Replace every bracket in the sample dialogue with your own records and genuine experience.

Who this brief is for

Prioritize creators who already use Oura, Apple Watch, WHOOP or another sleep-recording device and can share their own dated records. Suitable scenes include screen-heavy remote work, office breaks, hotels and travel. Creators who wear lash extensions can test the 3D eye-cup fit. Fitness and recovery creators are also welcome when they genuinely track their rest.

What the video should show

Include a real screen recording of the responsive mode/app and at least one clearly labelled within-session reading or tracker summary. Use actual before/after values only if recorded; the meeting’s numerical examples are not expected results. Record fit, 3D eye-cup space and actual warmth/audio use as well.

Present these as personal device records, not proof of causation or a guaranteed outcome. Do not say a change was caused by the product based on this log alone. The brand must review any proposed sleep-benefit claim against matching product evidence before publication; a disclaimer does not make an unsupported claim acceptable.

Return the evidence with the video

Send the daily log, dated original screenshots/screen recordings, complete comparison summary and the raw measurement footage, together with the approved video, clean export, agreed raw footage and at least five original photos. Redact unrelated personal details. Agree website/advertising reuse of your records before filming.
